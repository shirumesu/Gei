# Exploration

Use this reference for an open-ended landscape, trend, or "what exists?" question.

1. Define the useful boundary of the landscape.
2. Map representative categories and the major dimensions on which they differ.
3. Use strong sources for foundational facts and diverse accountable sources for interpretation.
4. Identify genuine consensus, active disputes, emerging alternatives, and missing evidence.
5. Stop when new sources repeat known categories or positions without changing the map.

Do not mistake the loudest sources for the whole field. Keep niche exceptions proportionate unless they alter the user's decision or reveal a hidden boundary.
