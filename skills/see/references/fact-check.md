# Fact Check

Use this reference for a disputed, precise, or consequential claim.

1. Rewrite the claim so its actor, action, date, region, version, population, and threshold are explicit where relevant.
2. Find the strongest original or accountable source.
3. Trace secondary reports back to their provenance and collapse repeated copies into one evidence chain.
4. Search for the qualifier or counterexample most likely to narrow or falsify the claim.
5. State a verdict in terms the evidence supports: confirmed, contradicted, partly true, outdated, misleading, or unresolved.

Keep the evidence for the core claim separate from context and inference. A source can be authentic yet fail to support the wording, scope, or causal conclusion being checked.

When the answer remains unresolved, identify the missing record, observation, or authority that would change the verdict.
