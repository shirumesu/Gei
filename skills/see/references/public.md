# Public Posts And User Voice

Use this reference when forums, reviews, social posts, or community discussion are evidence.

Public material can establish that particular people reported, believed, or experienced something. It cannot by itself establish prevalence, representativeness, causation, or objective product behavior.

## Sampling

- State the platforms, time window, search terms, and obvious access limits when they shape the sample.
- Look across distinct communities when the claim concerns broad sentiment.
- Collapse reposts, coordinated campaigns, copied reviews, and common upstream sources.
- Notice selection bias: people who post may be unusually enthusiastic, dissatisfied, expert, or incentivized.
- Preserve meaningful minority experiences without presenting them as population estimates.

Use official or technical evidence to verify objective claims discovered in posts. Quote only the smallest excerpt needed and keep the author's context intact.

Report sentiment as observed themes and counter-themes, not as a survey unless the data actually supports population inference.
